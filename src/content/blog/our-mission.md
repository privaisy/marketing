In today's AI-driven world, businesses are faced with a choice: figure out how to leverage AI to optimize productivity in a safe, secure manner or get left behind by their competition.

Navigating this landscape isn't easy, but private AI infrastructure _is_ accessible to organizations of any size. Privaisy is here to offer your business the expertise it needs to forge ahead in this new future.

## Our Mission

Privaisy's number one goal is to help businesses of any size to utilize private, secure AI infrastructure without complexity, per-seat licenses, or exposing the business's proprietary information, trade secrets, or secure data to the outside world.

Public AI tools cannot offer these guarantees, but Privaisy can take you there.

### Key Pillars

1. **Privacy-First** - the data you provide your AI stays on infrastructure accessible only to your organization. This means nobody is selling your data, no giant AI companies are using your data to train their models, and none of your sensitive business data is showing up in AI responses.
2. **Simplicity** - Privaisy has developed the playbook to make this successful for your organization. No dedicated IT staff is required, instead Privaisy handles the launch, maintenance, and upgrading of your solution for you.
3. **Pricing Transparency** - Our pricing model is for your entire organization, not per seat. This gives you predictable pricing and allows you to make well-informed decisions about your business's use of AI.
4. **Compliance** - Privaisy will work with you to make sure you meet the regulatory standards your business operates under to maintain the proper compliance position.
5. **Partnership** - Our team's focus is to make private AI easy for your company, managing the infrastructure so you can focus on your operations. That doesn't mean we disappear, though. Instead, our team is on standby to help you with any technology projects you may need assistance with.

## Real-World Impact

Privaisy was created to empower every organization to harness the productivity benefits of AI without compromising the business. AI should be working _for_ your organization - under your team's control. This results in your team moving faster while your business maintains ownership of its data and intellectual property.

Don't choose between innovation and security - get both with Privaisy.
